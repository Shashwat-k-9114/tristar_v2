The Kalinga Kitchen

It began with Smt. Anuri.

The recipe has been carried down in our family from my great-grandmother.

Every dish at The Kalinga Kitchen follows the cooking she handed down — the spice blends, the slow pots, the Odia rasoi as it was meant to be eaten. We have kept it that way, and opened our doors so you can taste it.
